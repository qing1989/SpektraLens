Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production Android app launcher icon foreground for the local photo-editing app 光谱暗房 / SPEKTRA.
Primary request: Design one beautiful original, immediately recognizable emblem: a single broad strip of photographic film gracefully folded into a compact upright capital S. The continuous strip has two open opposing curves, with a few precise rectangular sprocket perforations along its outer film edge. The S silhouette should read clearly at 48 pixels, conveying analog photography, spectral light and a small personal darkroom.
Style/medium: refined tactile editorial product-icon render, restrained shallow three-dimensional folded translucent film, graphic clarity and crisp silhouette. Not a photoreal camera or a text character typed in a font.
Color palette: luminous burnt orange and warm amber photographic emulsion, deep orange shaded folds, small warm cream edge highlights. Main app UI is charcoal black and vivid orange; the emblem must look excellent on a near-black background.
Composition/framing: exactly one centered isolated emblem on a genuinely transparent square canvas, front view, balanced negative space. S mark occupies about 70 percent of canvas height and 58 percent width. All film perforations must be true transparent holes. Keep both loops thick and optically balanced, with no delicate strands or tiny detail.
Materials/textures: subtle fine analog-film texture only within the material, glossy translucent amber edge catching soft light, no bloom extending far beyond the silhouette.
Text: none. The emblem shape itself evokes an S but there must be no lettering, title, numbers, labels or watermark.
Constraints: no surrounding icon tile, no baked rounded-square border, no mockup, no camera lens, no reels, no mountains, no stars, no rainbow swirl, no scattered elements, no cast shadow on an imaginary background. Transparent background and transparent sprocket holes. Output a single finished high-quality square foreground asset, not a concept sheet.
