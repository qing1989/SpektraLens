Source page: https://grlife.app/film-bay/index.html?lang=zh-hans
manifest.json records every source URL, original SHA-256, and exact stock mapping.
All 11 downloaded 120x120 PNGs are kept byte-for-byte unchanged in grlife/.

The 11 label PNGs cover 13 configurations. Portra 800 and its two push variants
share one label; the native renderer adds +1 / +2 markers for those variants.
Seven stocks without an exact matching public icon retain original vector art.

For these label assets the spool, metallic rims, film leader and selection marker
are drawn by FilmBadge.java. Optional complete upright cartridge artwork can
still be installed as <stock-id>.png for an otherwise unmapped stock.
